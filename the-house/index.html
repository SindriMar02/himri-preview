Before you arrive.

Himri is in Kjós, approximately 30 minutes by car from the capital region. A car is needed to reach the villa. Free parking is available at the house.

The entire villa is for your group. Self check-in is available. Exact arrival instructions and house rules are provided through your confirmed reservation.

A kitchen, Wi-Fi, a workspace, a sauna, a hot tub, a gym and games room. The sunroom connects to the dining room and terrace.

Choose your dates and guests, send a request, and the host confirms within a day with a secure link to pay. Minimum stay two nights.
